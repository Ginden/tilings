# Implement Labbé’s 19 Wang tiles

Add the self-similar nineteen-tile Wang shift and its recognizable two-dimensional morphism.

## Acceptance criteria

- Encode all nineteen Wang tiles, edge colors, and the published primitive expansive morphism.
- Generate patches by supertile substitution with every adjacency locally valid.
- Preserve unique composition and expose the hierarchy through tile classes or decoration.
- Test the full set, morphism, edge legality, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Cite the tile table and every morphism image used by the implementation; attribute reused assets and verify license compatibility.
- Document its minimal/self-similar relationship to the Jeandel–Rao core; typecheck, tests, and build pass.

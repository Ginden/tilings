# Implement Danzer–van Ophuysen golden triangles

Add the right-golden-triangle substitution with its finite colored/oriented forcing states.

- Implement the two triangle sizes and standard ten-state colored/oriented local rule from the published construction.

- Generate a gap-free requested patch with all required states.
- Test similarity ratios, substitution area, crowns/classes, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Document the chair/A2 duality and sofic forcing result; typecheck, tests, and build pass.

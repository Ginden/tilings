# Implement Danzer–van Ophuysen golden triangles

Add the right-golden-triangle substitution with its finite colored/oriented forcing states.

## Acceptance criteria

- Implement the two triangle sizes and standard ten-state colored/oriented local rule from the published construction.
- Preserve the substitution and vertex-crown constraints; do not reuse the chair geometry as a visual proxy.
- Generate a gap-free requested patch with all required states.
- Test similarity ratios, substitution area, crowns/classes, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Cite the source page/figure for all colored states and crown constraints; attribute reused assets and verify license compatibility.
- Document the chair/A2 duality and sofic forcing result; typecheck, tests, and build pass.

## References

- Danzer and van Ophuysen, “A species of planar triangular tilings with inflation factor √−τ” (2001) — original construction; see the full bibliographic record in the local catalog.
- [Vereshchagin, “A family of non-periodic tilings of the plane by right golden triangles”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.10658) — modern forcing treatment.
- [Tilings Encyclopedia: Golden Triangle](https://tilings.math.uni-bielefeld.de/substitution/golden-triangle/) — substitution diagram and geometric summary.

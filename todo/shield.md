# Implement the Gähler Shield tiling

Add the dodecagonal Shield presentation using squares, equilateral triangles, and marked shield tiles.

## Acceptance criteria

- Transcribe the self-similar Shield rule and its four standard marked/oriented prototile states.
- Preserve the vertex-cross matching information and twelvefold hierarchy.
- Generate a gap-free patch covering the requested disc.
- Test substitution area, all states, legal geometry, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Document its model-set status and MLD relation to Socolar; typecheck, tests, and build pass.

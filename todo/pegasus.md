# Implement Pegasus tiles

Add Goodman-Strauss’s two-tile aperiodic system with its tip-to-tip matching rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- Transcribe both polygonal prototiles and the native tip-to-tip constraints from the archival Pegasus source.
- Preserve the forced hierarchy rather than producing a periodic packing of the bare shapes.
- Generate a gap-free patch covering the requested disc.
- Test both prototiles, legal tip contacts, hierarchy, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Cite the exact archival figures used for outlines and matching marks; attribute reused assets and verify license compatibility.
- Document the construction and source; typecheck, tests, and build pass.

## References

- [Goodman-Strauss, “The Pegasus Tiles: an aperiodic pair of tiles”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.07166) — primary construction and hierarchy proof.
- [Goodman-Strauss archival “Penrose Tiles” notes](https://strauss.hosted.uark.edu/distribution/tilings/penhex.pdf) — earlier drawings and construction context.

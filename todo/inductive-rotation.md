# Implement the Inductive Rotation tiling

Add the Hofstetter–Frettlöh fourfold inductive-rotation substitution.

## Acceptance criteria

- Transcribe the published polygonal/decorated prototiles and inductive rotation rule.
- Preserve successively rotated supertiles and the limit-periodic hierarchy.
- Generate a gap-free patch covering the requested disc.
- Test rotation progression, substitution area, classes, coverage, rendering, and registry integration.
- Cite the exact source rule and the angle sequence used by the implementation; attribute reused assets and verify license compatibility.
- Document its pure-point/limit-periodic status; typecheck, tests, and build pass.
